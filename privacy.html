Privacy Policy — Garmin AI Coach

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Garmin AI Coach is a personal, non-commercial application operated for its developer's own fitness and training analysis. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the app obtains Garmin activity data and wellness metrics, which can include workouts, heart rate, sleep, and training-readiness information.

Google Drive access and storage

The app uses Google Drive authorization to upload and update its generated Garmin data snapshots in a private Google Drive folder controlled by the account owner. The app does not require public access to the owner's Drive files.

Use and sharing

Data is used to maintain personal training records and support fitness analysis. The operator does not sell personal data or publish Garmin health records on this website. Data passes through the services used for the automation, including Garmin, GitHub Actions, and Google Drive, subject to their respective service terms.

Retention and control

Stored data remains in the account owner's Google Drive until deleted by the account owner. Google access can be revoked in the owner's Google Account security settings. The GitHub repository containing this public website does not contain personal training data or authentication secrets.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact lbaskys@gmail.com.
